"Build the Smart City of the Future"

Each team acts as a technology company. They receive a fixed budget and participate in an auction to buy technology assets. After the auction, they must use those assets to design a functional smart city on a virtual map provided by the organizers.

Duration: 2.5 hours

Introduction & Rules – 15 min
Auction – 60–75 min
City Planning – 45 min
Presentation – 20–25 min
Result – 10 min
Objective

Purchase the best combination of technology components and build the most efficient, secure, and sustainable smart city.

Team Details
Team Size: 4–6 students
Number of Teams: 8–15
Starting Budget: ₹10 Crore (Virtual Money)

No real money is involved.

Host Script (Sample)

"Welcome to the Tech Auction! Today, you are technology companies competing to build the smartest city. Each team starts with ₹10 Crore in virtual currency. Bid wisely because every technology asset will directly impact your city's performance. After the auction, you'll use your purchases to design and present a fully functional smart city. Strategic thinking, not just spending, will determine the winner. Let the bidding begin!"

Tips to Make the Event More Engaging
Display each item with an image, specifications, and a one-line real-world use before bidding.
Introduce surprise event cards throughout the auction to force teams to think about resilience and security.
Allow teams to trade one item with another team during a short 5-minute negotiation window after the auction (optional).
Give "hidden bonus" points for well-balanced infrastructure instead of simply buying the most expensive items.
Use realistic constraints, such as requiring every hospital, school, and government office to have network connectivity, making planning more meaningful.
