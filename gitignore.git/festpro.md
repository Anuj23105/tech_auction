## 2. INTRODUCTION

Technology is the backbone of modern civilization, and cultivating a strong technical culture among students is the need of the hour. TechNova is an initiative by the TechNight Club to create a platform where students can showcase their skills, learn from industry professionals, collaborate, compete, and grow.

TechNova is not just an event — it is a movement to bring the spirit of innovation alive within the walls of Shri Balaji Disha College and extend it to the broader student community across Raipur and beyond.

---

## 3. ABOUT TECHNOVA

**TechNova** (Technology + Nova — Latin for "New") represents the dawn of a new era of technical excellence at Shri Balaji Disha College.

- **Organized by:** TechNight Club, CS Department
- **Motto:** *"Code. Create. Conquer."*
- **Scope:** Intra-college (Day 1 & 2) + Inter-college (Day 3)
- **Edition:** Inaugural / 1st Edition

---

## 4. THEME OF THE EVENT

> **"Building the Future, One Line at a Time"**

The theme focuses on the idea that every great technological advancement begins with a single step — a line of code, an idea, a team. TechNova encourages students to take that first step confidently.

---

## 5. NEED AND PURPOSE OF THE EVENT

- Students in technical colleges often lack exposure to real-world industry practices.
- Competitive events sharpen problem-solving and analytical thinking.
- Inter-college hackathons promote networking and collaboration beyond campus walls.
- Guest talks from industry professionals bridge the gap between academics and the industry.
- Events like Tech Quiz and Treasure Hunt make learning engaging and memorable.

---

## 6. OBJECTIVES

1. Provide a competitive platform for showcasing technical skills.
2. Expose students to current industry trends through expert interaction.
3. Encourage teamwork, creativity, and innovative thinking.
4. Promote inter-college collaboration and healthy competition.
5. Build the reputation of Shri Balaji Disha College as a hub of technical activity.
6. Identify and nurture technically talented students.

---

## 7. EXPECTED LEARNING OUTCOMES

- Improved problem-solving and critical thinking skills.
- Hands-on experience in team collaboration under time constraints.
- Exposure to real-world applications of classroom learning.
- Increased confidence in presenting and defending ideas.
- Awareness of current technology trends from industry professionals.
- Experience in competing at inter-college level.

## 17. RISK MANAGEMENT AND SAFETY MEASURES

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Low student turnout | Early promotion, incentives (certificates, prizes, extra credit) |
| Technical failure (internet, power) | Backup power (UPS/generator), offline fallback plans |
| Guest speaker cancellation | Confirm 2 weeks in advance, keep a backup speaker |
| Insufficient registrations from outside colleges | Target 10 colleges to ensure 5 confirmed |
| Budget shortfall | Confirm sponsorships before finalizing prize amounts |
| Cheating in quiz/hackathon | Strict jury, code-of-conduct agreement at registration |
| COVID/Health emergency | Follow college health protocols, sanitization at entry |
